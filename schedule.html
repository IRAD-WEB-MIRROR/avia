Flight schedule

Flights of Iradiya (Irad Air Services) from the Talas Civil Air Terminal. Times are local: Talas and Tash-Kömür on Irad Time (UTC+6), Almaty and Tashkent UTC+5, Dubai UTC+4, Istanbul UTC+3. Days: 1 = Monday … 7 = Sunday.

International flights are charter flights. Each flight needs landing and overflight permits from the countries concerned. Times can change at short notice, and a flight can be postponed if a permit has not arrived. The terminal does not sell tickets; for bookings and changes contact Iradiya.

Other flights

Iradiya's Twin Otter flights to Toktogul, Kerben and Kazarman, medical evacuation flights and flights for the United Nations Mission in Irad are arranged individually and do not appear on the public schedule. Cargo is handled by the Talas Airport Logistics Hub next to the terminal.
