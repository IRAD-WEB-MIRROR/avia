Press service news

Announcements of the Talas Civil Air Terminal, newest first.

2026

  • 28 October 2026 · № 41
    Southern apron works, 3–7 November

    From 3 to 7 November the stands in front of the terminal are closed for joint sealing. Passengers will be taken to the aircraft by bus from exit 3. Please come to boarding without delay when it is announced.

  • 25 October 2026
    Winter schedule in force

    The winter schedule runs from 25 October 2026 to 27 March 2027. From 1 December to 15 March the Tash-Kömür flight is daily. See the schedule.

  • 18 August 2026
    Extra flights to Tash-Kömür for the Feast of St. Janyl

    Iradiya adds flights to Tash-Kömür on 21 and 26 August for pilgrims. Seats are sold only at Iradiya's office, Liberty Boulevard 112.

  • 27 March 2026
    Summer schedule 2026

    From 29 March the Tash-Kömür flight returns to three a week (Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday). The Almaty, Dubai and Istanbul charters continue; Tashkent flights operate as permits are received.

  • 20 January 2026
    About 41,000 passengers in 2025

    The terminal handled about 41,000 passengers in 2025, most of them on the Tash-Kömür route. The busiest day was 22 August, before the Feast of St. Janyl.

2024

  • 24 October 2024
    Istanbul flight: technical stop at Baku

    From the winter schedule the Friday Istanbul flight makes a technical stop at Baku for fuel. Passengers stay on board; no passengers are taken on or set down at Baku.

2023

  • 5 June 2023
    Departure hall reopened

    After two months of repairs the departure hall on the first floor is open again, with new seating for 120, the café Ak Kanat and a Talasbank exchange counter.

2022

  • 28 November 2022
    Daily flights to Tash-Kömür in winter

    While snow closes the mountain road, the Tash-Kömür flight will be daily from 1 December to 15 March. Residents of Lower Naryn District pay half fare on presenting their residence record.

2021

  • 22 November 2021
    Fog at Talas: arrivals may divert to Aulie-Ata

    In winter fog, arrivals that cannot land at Talas divert to the Aulie-Ata airfield. Passengers are brought to Talas by bus; the information desk gives the time of arrival.

2019

  • 23 October 2019
    Terminal charge from the winter schedule

    From 27 October 2019 a terminal charge of 50 som (domestic) and 600 som (international) is collected in cash at check-in.
