Contacts and reception

The information desk answers around the clock. Tickets are sold only by Iradiya, not at the terminal. Complaints about service in the terminal may be left in the book at the information desk or sent by e-mail; they are answered within 30 days.

Office hours

The terminal opens two and a half hours before the first departure and closes after the last arrival. Iradiya ticket office: Monday to Friday 9:00–18:00, Saturday 9:00–14:00.
