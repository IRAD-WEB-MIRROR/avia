Announcements

28 October 2026 · No. 41
Southern apron works, 3–7 November

From 3 to 7 November the stands in front of the terminal are closed for joint sealing. Passengers will be taken to the aircraft by bus from exit 3. Please come to boarding without delay when it is announced.

K. Rakhmatova, duty manager

25 October 2026 · No. 40
Winter schedule in force

The winter schedule runs from 25 October 2026 to 27 March 2027. Talas – Tash-Kömür: Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, daily from 1 December to 15 March. Almaty on Wednesdays and Saturdays, Tashkent on Thursdays, Dubai on Mondays, Istanbul on Fridays. See the schedule.

Civil Aviation Directorate

20 October 2026 · No. 38
Photography on the Airfield Road

We remind passengers and those meeting them that photography and filming are forbidden on the Airfield Road, at the South Checkpoint and of the airfield, including from the terminal windows. Cameras and telephones may be checked. Do not stop on the Airfield Road outside the marked places.

Civil Aviation Directorate

12 October 2026 · No. 36
Tashkent flights

Flights IRD 741/742 Talas – Tashkent operate subject to the permit for each flight. If the permit has not arrived by the day before, the flight is postponed and Iradiya informs passengers by telephone. Please leave a working telephone number when buying your ticket.

Talas – Tash-Kömür

The only scheduled air route in Irad, flown by Iradiya with ATR 72 aircraft: Tuesdays, Thursdays and Saturdays, and every day from 1 December to 15 March, when snow closes the Ala-Bel pass. The flight takes one hour against seven by road.

Next flights: 3 November 2026, 5 November 2026, 7 November 2026

One way 380 som; residents of Lower Naryn District 190 som with a residence record. Tickets from Iradiya offices only.
