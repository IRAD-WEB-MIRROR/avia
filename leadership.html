Leadership

The terminal is run by the Civil Aviation Directorate of the Irad Special Economic Zone, part of the Ministry of Transport. The duty manager is responsible for the terminal at any hour.

Kamila Rakhmatova

Kamila Rakhmatova

Duty manager of the terminal

Responsible for the terminal, check-in and boarding, passenger information and the announcements of the terminal. Delays, cancellations and diversions are announced over her signature.

Deputies and officials

Minister of Transport (the Directorate is part of the Ministry) · since 2018

Head of the Civil Aviation Directorate
