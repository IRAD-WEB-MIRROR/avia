Documents and legal acts

Rules that apply to passengers and to everyone on the Airfield Road and in the terminal.

Order of the Gendarmerie · 28 October 2024 · JG-17

Photography on the Airfield Road and at the South Checkpoint

Photographing the checkpoint, the base perimeter and military aircraft is prohibited. Photography inside the terminal is allowed, except at security screening.

Download (PDF) ›

Order of the Directorate · 1 March 2021 · CAD-2021/3

Rules for baggage and items carried in the cabin

Free baggage 20 kg on Iradiya's domestic flights and 23 kg on charters, cabin bag 5 kg; no liquids over 100 ml in the cabin on international flights; knives, kymyz in skins and live animals only as declared cargo.

Order of the Directorate · 16 September 2019 · CAD-2019/7

On the terminal charge

50 som for domestic and 600 som for international departures, in cash at check-in; children under two, crews and UNMI rotation passengers exempt.

Arrangement (summary) · 1 September 2016 · JUA 2016

Joint Use Arrangement for Talas Air Base

Sets the hours and procedures for civil flights at the joint civil–military airfield; the base control tower controls all traffic. The full text is not published.

Act · 10 June 1997 · № 97-14

Civil Aviation Act

Provides for the certification of airlines and crews, the register of civil aircraft and the civil terminal. Amended 2001 (terminal) and 2008 (the Directorate under the Ministry of Transport).
